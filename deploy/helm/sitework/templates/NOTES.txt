  kubectl -n {{ $ns }} get pods,svc,ingress -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

  kubectl -n {{ $ns }} logs deploy/{{ include "sitework.web.fullname" . }} -f

Note: the SecretStore and ExternalSecret are Helm hooks and the Secret "{{ include "sitework.db.secretName" . }}" is
orphaned on purpose, so `helm uninstall` leaves all three. Remove them with:
  kubectl -n {{ $ns }} delete externalsecret,secretstore -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
  kubectl -n {{ $ns }} delete secret {{ include "sitework.db.secretName" . }}
